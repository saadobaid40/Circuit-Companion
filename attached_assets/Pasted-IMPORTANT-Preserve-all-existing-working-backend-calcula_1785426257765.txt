### 3. Electrical Machinery Suite (New Tab):
1. DC Motor / Generator Analyzer:
   - Inputs: Terminal Voltage ($V_t$), Armature Resistance ($R_a$), Field Current, and Load.
   - Outputs: Back EMF ($E_a$), Speed (RPM), Developed Torque ($\tau$), and interactive Speed vs. Torque characteristic curve.
2. Induction Motor & Slip Calculator:
   - Inputs: Stator frequency ($f$), Poles ($P$), Actual Rotor Speed ($N$).
   - Outputs: Synchronous Speed ($N_s$), Slip ($s$), Rotor frequency ($f_r$), and a visual Power Flow Diagram.
3. Synchronous Machine Phasor Diagram Generator:
   - Compute Voltage Regulation ($\% \text{VR}$) and Torque Angle ($\delta$) with a vector phasor visualizer.
